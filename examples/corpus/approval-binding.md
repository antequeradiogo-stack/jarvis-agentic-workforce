# Binding approvals to requests (synthetic note)
A human approval should authorise one exact request, not a whole category of actions. Hashing the canonical action, parameters, run and task gives a fingerprint that the approval is bound to. If any of those change, the hash changes and the approval no longer matches. Approvals should expire quickly and be single-use, so a leaked or replayed approval is worthless. A signature over the approval detects tampering with the expiry or the bound hash.
